Frequently asked questions

How do I restore Premium?

Select Restore Purchases on the app’s Premium page, using the Apple Account that made the purchase and an internet connection. Restoration recovers eligible rights, not follows, pins or notification settings. Do not delete the app as a first troubleshooting step.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. Premium is a one-time, non-consumable purchase with no monthly fee. Apple supplies the actual storefront price; check the app and Apple confirmation screen. Available Premium features are unlimited followed countries, followed diseases and pinned announcements. Unfinished remote alerts and globe themes are not advertised as available.

Why does a statistic say “Not provided”?

The source may not publish the indicator, or the disease, location, case definition and period cannot be safely identified. Missing does not mean zero. Hong Kong monthly notification counts are not today’s new confirmed cases.

What if data cannot load?

Check your connection and retry. Review the data update time in the app. Cached data may be outdated; follow the original source link to verify. Keep your app data; reinstalling is not necessary.

Will I receive global live alerts with the app closed?

Production remote outbreak alerts and continuous monitoring while the app is closed are not available. Local test notifications only help check notification settings; they are not actual outbreak alerts.

Will follows and pins sync to another phone?

Version 1.0 has no app-managed iCloud or backend synchronization. Purchase rights and local lists are separate. Restoring purchases does not restore lists. System backups depend on your iOS settings.
