OUTBREAKNOW / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

How the app and website handle information.

Scope of this policy

This policy describes the iOS app and this website based on the OutbreakNow 1.0 release configuration. Operator display name (public brand): OutbreakNow. Privacy contact email: outbreaknow.support@gmail.com. Effective date: 2026-10-16.

01 · Information on your device

The app stores followed countries and diseases, pinned announcements and their order, notification preferences and history, notification deduplication records, an installation identifier, and cached official announcements, historical announcements and statistics locally. These support personalized reading, settings, notification management and offline display. They are not sent to a developer server. Version 1.0 requires no app account and provides no developer backend or app-managed iCloud synchronization.

02 · Apple purchases and restoration

Premium is a one-time, lifetime non-consumable in-app purchase, not a subscription. Apple handles payment and refunds. Purchases, transaction verification and restoration use Apple StoreKit. The app receives transaction and entitlement information to determine access; it does not receive your payment card number or send transaction proofs to a developer server. Refunds or revocations may remove entitlement. Apple Privacy Policy ↗

03 · Official data requests and external links

The app downloads public data directly over HTTPS from Taiwan CDC and Hong Kong’s Centre for Health Protection. These providers may receive your IP address and ordinary request metadata; their own policies govern retention and use. The app does not attach your follows, pins, notification history or purchase information to these requests. External source links are subject to the destination website’s policies. Taiwan CDC ↗ · Hong Kong CHP ↗

04 · Notifications and permissions

Notification permission is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings. Test notifications are scheduled locally and identified as tests. With notification permission and the notification switch enabled, the app may register with Apple APNs. The resulting device token is held in memory only and is not uploaded to a developer backend in version 1.0. Production remote outbreak alerts and continuous monitoring while the app is closed are not provided. The app does not access your location, contacts, photos or health data.

05 · Retention and your choices

Local settings generally remain until you change or remove them, or the system removes app data. Caches and notification records may be replaced as the app updates or manages storage. You can manage individual follows, pins and notification preferences in the app. Purchasing, restoring or losing Premium does not intentionally erase saved follows or pins; entries beyond free limits may become inactive. Deleting the app may lose local data. Restore Purchases restores eligible purchase rights, not these lists. iOS backups depend on your system settings; the app does not guarantee recovery after deletion.

06 · Website hosting and tracking

This website includes no advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, forms, cookies or browser storage. Language switching uses ordinary links. Once deployed to GitHub Pages, GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security, whether or not visitors are signed in. GitHub Pages data collection ↗ · GitHub Privacy Statement ↗

07 · Support and privacy requests

For support and privacy requests, email outbreaknow.support@gmail.com. The link attempts to open your configured email app; it does not send a message automatically. This website has no contact form. When you voluntarily email us, the operator and Gmail (Google) process your sender address, message and optional attachments to respond to your request, separately from local app data. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers or unnecessary health information. Google processes service data under its Privacy Policy ↗. The retention and deletion principles for support emails are described in the next section.

07a · Email retention and deletion

Support emails are used only to respond to inquiries and provide support. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed, and information that is no longer needed is reviewed periodically. You may request access to or deletion of your information by emailing outbreaknow.support@gmail.com. We handle these requests in accordance with applicable law. This policy does not promise an automatic deletion mechanism or a fixed response deadline, nor does it claim that Google system backups can be deleted immediately. Google system backups and retention practices are governed by its policies.

08 · Policy updates

If accounts, synchronization, remote notifications or other data processing are introduced, this policy will be updated with the necessary notices and choices before activation. This website does not replace App Store privacy disclosures. The submitted app, disclosures and policy must accurately match.
