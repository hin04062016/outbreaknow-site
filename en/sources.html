Read the definition, not just the number

Announcement date, statistical period and retrieval time are different. Sources may update on different schedules and revise historical values. A notification is not automatically a confirmed case. Announcement counts are not case counts; monthly notifications are not daily new cases. Values from different regions, diseases or periods are not combined into a global total.

Independent information, with limits

OutbreakNow is not an official product of any government or the WHO, and is not endorsed or sponsored by these organizations. Coverage is limited to published data from integrated sources; it is not comprehensive global surveillance. WHO Disease Outbreak News content is not automatically imported. Disease encyclopedia entries link to their cited official references in the app.

For information, not diagnosis

The app helps you read public health information. It does not provide diagnosis, personalized medical advice, emergency services or a guarantee of local safety. Check the latest official guidance and seek appropriate professional medical advice.

Attribution and corrections

Official data remains subject to its source terms, specific restrictions and third-party rights. Government names identify the sources, not an endorsement. No government or WHO logos are used on this website. If an error is found, email support and include the original source link.
